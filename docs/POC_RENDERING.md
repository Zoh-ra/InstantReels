## 4. Assessment

- **Performance**: acceptable for an asynchronous job (minutes, not hours). 4K is ~4× the 1080p cost.
- **Accuracy**: frame-exact scene timing, exact duration, A/V sync and background speed are verified by measurements, not assumed.
- **Maintenance**: ~600 lines of TypeScript (stills, FFmpeg graph, validation). The FFmpeg graph is the most delicate part and is covered by the validation suite.
- **Limits**: animation is limited to what FFmpeg applies to stills (fade-in used here; slide/scale possible). Per-frame custom animations would need a frame-by-frame engine.

**Recommendation: keep Playwright + FFmpeg.** No reason found to switch to Remotion.
